# Scientific scope

## Active target

The active configurations cover nine synthetic nonstationarity regimes, corrected original-paper
sweeps, modern comparators, the Safi wind-speed application, Safi sensitivity, limited-history and
correlation/boundary stress tests, controlled solver verification, and runtime/memory studies.

## Exclusions

The active profiles do not rerun appliance-energy or hurricane experiments. Energy matrices remain
archival; weakly regularized robust-norm candidates were not reliably certified in the supplied
energy setup. The two historical hurricane matrices are absent.

A successful artifact verification therefore means that the configured **synthetic + Safi** scope is
complete. It does not mean that every application in an older manuscript has been regenerated.

## Claim discipline

The expanded evidence supports a conditional conclusion: the affine error-history rule is strongest
when relative-reliability patterns recur or evolve predictably, and can be inferior to short-memory
recursive methods under unprecedented abrupt changes. Public documentation and manuscripts should
not describe the method as universally superior or universally robust to all drift.
