# Synthetic + Safi scope audit — 2026-07-30

## Executive conclusion

The appliance-energy application is removed from the active reviewer-response experiment
profile. The exact robust-norm fallback for the weakly regularized energy candidate returned
Gurobi status `INTERRUPTED` after the preceding IRLS fit failed to meet its numerical
certificate. Continuing to widen stopping tolerances or repeatedly invoking a large exact
SOCP would make the release slower and less auditable without being necessary to answer
most reviewer questions.

The stable active scope is therefore:

- expanded synthetic drift regimes, 30 seeds;
- corrected original-paper synthetic sweeps, 30 seeds;
- Julia-native neural and boosted meta-learner comparison;
- Safi held-out evaluation and moving-block bootstrap;
- Safi hyperparameter sensitivity for the primary smooth Adaptive Ridge estimator;
- synthetic limited-history, correlated-member, pruning, and boundary tests;
- controlled synthetic solver equivalence checks;
- controlled runtime and memory benchmarks.

## Why the remaining scope is scientifically useful

The completed expanded synthetic experiment directly addresses abrupt changes, periodic
drift, persistent bias, member degradation, recurring regimes, and variance bursts. The
manuscript sweeps address window size, training-history length, member count, drift level,
and intermittent drift. The Safi application gives one real chronological use case with
modern baselines and moving-block inference.

This evidence is enough to answer major reviewer requests concerning:

- additional drift processes;
- modern dynamic and meta-learning baselines;
- sensitivity to history window and regularization;
- limited historical data;
- correlated members and pruning;
- computational effort;
- boundary and cold-start policies;
- fair chronological tuning.

It is not enough to retain claims that the revised experiments validate appliance energy or
hurricane intensity forecasting.

## Preservation of completed results

The new migration utility verifies and preserves only:

```text
legacy_audit
synthetic_full
manuscript_sweeps
synthetic_meta
real_world_safi
```

For each stage it checks:

- the source run identity;
- the stage marker fingerprint;
- every required output SHA-256 hash;
- exact seed/regime/method grid size;
- finite test metrics;
- the Safi method set;
- certification of every Safi robust-norm validation candidate.

It copies no energy output, failure log, or serialized model.

study. Any abstract, introduction, contribution, conclusion, table, or checklist statement
